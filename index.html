Proof of concept

C major scale assessor

Plays a metronome count-in, records the microphone, then scores each note of a one-octave C major scale for timing and intonation.

Use headphones if you can, otherwise the metronome may bleed into the microphone and muddy the result.

Ready. The app expects: C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5, one note per beat.

Expected notes

Each box turns green when it is in time and in tune, amber when only one dimension passes, and red when it misses.
